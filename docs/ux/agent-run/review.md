# Adversarial review of the agent-run designs

Workstream A's review record, 2026-11-12. It covers everything in this folder: README, personas,
story, journeys, approval cards, information architecture and the eight journey frame files.

## Method

One reviewer, briefed to break the design rather than polish it, walked every persona (P1 to P9) and
both adversaries (X1, a hostile page, and X2, a rogue or hijacked agent) through J1 to J8. It
checked each step against the plan, `docs/security-model.md`, `docs/relay.md`,
`docs/authoring-agent.md` and the v3 personas and IA, and looked for three things: a way for X1 or X2
to get a change past the person without an honest card, a place where a real person (thumb first,
large text, VoiceOver, away from the Mac) would get stuck or misled, and a place where the
documents and frames disagree.

It found **75 findings: 24 Must fix, 33 Should fix and 18 Minor**. Nick's answers on the main
trade-offs (which gestures need biometrics, the lost-phone route, the store, Undo, notification
actions, consent on the phone) were applied across docs, story and frames together, so the world
stays one world.

Dispositions:

- **Fixed:** the docs or frames now say or show what the finding asked for; the cell says where.
- **Changed design:** fixed by changing the design rather than the wording; the new rule is named.
- **For B / D / F / G / H:** the design is drawn, and the named workstream owns the mechanism.
- **Question for Nick:** listed in `information-architecture.md`, section 9.
- **Open:** not done in this pass.

Short forms: AC approval-cards.md, J journeys.md, IA information-architecture.md, ST story.md.

**Update, 2026-11-12:** Nick answered open question 3: the passcode always works, on every
approval. That reverses the biometrics-only part of findings 1, 2, 25 and 35 below by his choice;
the Mac-only list and lost-phone removal stay.
